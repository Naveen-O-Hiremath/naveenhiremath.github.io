I don't just keep systems running.

01 — About

Secure infrastructure, automated operations.

A profile built on cloud administration, identity governance and relentless automation.

I'm an IT & Cloud Administrator and Automation Engineer based in Bengaluru, currently running cloud and endpoint operations at Hector Beverages (Paper Boat). My work sits at the intersection of three things: keeping an organisation's identity and endpoint estate secure, making the cloud spend defensible, and removing the manual toil that quietly drains an IT team.

Over 3+ years across Microsoft 365, Azure, AWS and GCP, I've administered Entra ID, Conditional Access, MFA and RBAC at scale, hardened endpoints through Intune and Defender, and driven vulnerability management and patch compliance against security benchmarks. On the response side, I investigate alerts across cloud and endpoints and manage platforms like CrowdStrike and Kaspersky.

What I enjoy most is the automation layer — PowerShell, Power Automate and Python turning onboarding, licensing, asset tracking and reporting into workflows that run themselves. I back all of it with thorough SOPs, runbooks and knowledge bases, so the process survives after I hand it over.
